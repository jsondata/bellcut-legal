BellCut Support

BellCut turns a video, audio file, or voice recording into an iPhone ringtone. Your download includes unlimited exports.

Make a ringtone

Choose a clip, drag the waveform handles to select up to 30 seconds, then tap Export. On iOS 26 or later, choose Use as Ringtone in the share sheet. On earlier iOS versions, save to Files, import the clip into GarageBand, then share the project as a ringtone. Apple’s GarageBand instructions.

Use your sound for an alarm

After adding your ringtone, open Clock, tap Alarms, choose a regular alarm, tap Sound, select your tone, and save the alarm.

Microphone access

To record your voice, allow microphone access when prompted. You can change this later in Settings under Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Contact support

Open a BellCut support request on GitHub. Include your iOS version and the step where you need help. Requests are public, so please leave out personal recordings and account details.
