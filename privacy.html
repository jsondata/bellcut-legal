Privacy Policy

BellCut processes the clips you choose on your iPhone. You can select a video in Photos, choose a file, or record with the microphone. Microphone access is requested when you tap Record.

Your clips

Audio used for editing and export is stored in the app’s temporary storage. iOS may clear temporary files. Copies you save to Files, share, or install as ringtones stay in the destinations you choose and can be removed there.

Data and purchases

BellCut does not collect your media, track you, or include advertising or analytics SDKs. Apple handles the purchase of the app.

Support and privacy questions

This website is hosted by GitHub. If you submit a support request on GitHub, your issue is public. Please keep private recordings and account details out of your message. Contact BellCut support.
